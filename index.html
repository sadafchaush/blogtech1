Why Learning HTML Still Matters

Posted by SADAF · August 2026

HTML is the foundation of every website you have ever visited. Before frameworks, before JavaScript, there was structure — and that structure is what HTML provides.

Every developer, no matter how advanced, started here. Understanding the basics well makes everything after it easier.

Here is what you should master first:

  • Headings and paragraphs
  • Lists and links
  • Images and semantic tags
